### Coffee and agriculture

Timor-Leste produces high-quality shade-grown arabica coffee, recognised in specialty markets around the world. Opportunities exist for collaboration with New Zealand in agri-technology, dairy farming, sustainable land management, food production and agricultural education.

### Tourism and marine

Atauro Island’s marine environment and Timor-Leste’s cultural landscape position the country as a compelling eco-tourism destination. Partnerships must develop with care for community benefit, cultural integrity and environmental protection.

### Infrastructure and digital

Roads, ports, energy systems and public facilities remain national priorities. The existing site’s subsea cable distance and completion claims require verification before publication, but the direction is clear: stronger connectivity can unlock education, public services and enterprise.

### Education and training

With a notably young population, demand for quality education, vocational training and English-language instruction is growing. New Zealand’s education sector is well placed to contribute meaningfully.

### Labour mobility

Timor-Leste participates in New Zealand’s **Recognised Seasonal Employer (RSE)** scheme — an active labour mobility relationship already in place. This creates genuine community links that can grow naturally into broader relationships over time.

### Energy and Greater Sunrise

Negotiations over the Greater Sunrise gas field represent one of the region’s most significant energy questions. Any investment language must remain measured and reflect current official information.
