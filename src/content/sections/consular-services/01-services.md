The Honorary Consulate serves Auckland and the North Island north of Wellington. It is a voluntary appointment and provides a practical first point of contact, guidance and referral.

The Consulate does **not** issue visas, passports or official consular documents. Those enquiries must go directly to the Embassy of Timor-Leste in Wellington.
